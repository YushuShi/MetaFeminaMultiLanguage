PMID: 41847477; PMCID: PMC12989687
Title: Association of Smoking and Alcohol Consumption with Female Reproductive Diseases: A Mendelian Randomization Study on the Mediating Role of Circulating Metabolites.
Context: Alcohol | Ovarian cancer | Incidence
Published date: 2026
Estimate: OR 0.48 (95% CI 0.31–0.74); p=0.001
Comparison: Higher genetically predicted drinks per week versus lower genetically predicted drinks per week
Reason kept separate: This two-sample Mendelian-randomization study reported a quantitative association between genetically predicted weekly alcohol consumption and endometrioid ovarian cancer risk and should be recorded separately from conventional studies.
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41847477/

PMID: 42183404; PMCID: PMC13197606
